# EP-001 — Kallanai: Guiding the River

**Working series title:** Prajan's Time Travel  
**Episode topic:** Kallanai (Grand Anicut), Kaveri River, Tamil Nadu  
**Format:** Family-friendly educational adventure  
**Target duration:** 3–4 minutes  
**Language draft:** English master script; Tamil version can be adapted scene-by-scene  
**Status:** Script/production planning

## Learning goal

Children should understand that Kallanai is an ancient stone water-diversion structure traditionally attributed to Karikala Chola, built to guide Kaveri water towards irrigation channels and support farming in the delta.

## Historical accuracy notes

- Kallanai stands across the Kaveri in Tamil Nadu and is traditionally associated with Karikala Chola.
- Institutional heritage material places its construction in the 2nd century CE and describes its purpose as diverting river water for irrigation.
- Explain it as an **anicut / water-diversion structure**, not simply as a modern storage dam.
- The present structure has seen later modifications. Avoid presenting a precise ancient construction method, exact dialogue, or detailed scene of the original building process as documented fact.
- Prajan's time travel, meeting with an engineer, and conversation with the king are fictional storytelling devices. Add a brief end-card: “Historical setting based on research; conversations and some scenes are imaginative reconstructions.”

## Story outline

1. **Modern-day hook:** Prajan visits Kallanai and wonders how people guided a mighty river long before modern machines.
2. **Time travel:** A warm golden glow transports him to an imagined ancient Kaveri riverbank.
3. **Meet the engineer:** Prajan meets an experienced worker/engineer who explains the goal: guide water towards fields, not stop the river completely.
4. **Water demonstration:** Using a small model of a river, channels, and fields, the engineer shows how a low barrier can change the direction and spread of water.
5. **Meet the king:** Prajan meets Karikala Chola in a respectful, imagined court scene. The king explains that good leadership should help people and protect the prosperity of the land. Keep this as fictional dialogue, not a historical quotation.
6. **Return to today:** Prajan returns to modern Kallanai and shares what he learned.

## Scene-by-scene script

### Scene 1 — The question (20–25 sec)
**Visual:** Present-day Kallanai, flowing Kaveri water. Prajan looks at the stone structure.

**Prajan:** “Wow! This huge stone structure is standing in the river. Who built it? And how did they do it so long ago?”

**Narrator:** “This is Kallanai, also called the Grand Anicut. It is an ancient water-diversion structure on the Kaveri River.”

### Scene 2 — The time-travel flashback (15–20 sec)
**Visual:** A gentle golden light surrounds Prajan. The modern scene transitions into an imagined ancient riverbank. Keep the transition magical but not frightening.

**Prajan:** “Whoa! Where am I?”

**Narrator:** “Prajan had travelled back in time, to an imagined scene from the ancient Chola period.”

### Scene 3 — Meet the river engineer (35–40 sec)
**Visual:** An experienced ancient Tamil engineer stands near the river with workers and stones. No dangerous construction action close to Prajan.

**Engineer:** “Welcome, little one. We are working to guide the river water towards the fields.”

**Prajan:** “But why not build a wall and stop all the water?”

**Engineer:** “The river gives life to the land. We must guide its water carefully, so farmers can grow crops.”

### Scene 4 — The water model (35–40 sec)
**Visual:** The engineer demonstrates with a small clay-and-sand model: a river channel, a low stone barrier, side channels, and tiny fields. Water flows gently into the channels.

**Engineer:** “Look closely. The barrier changes the flow. Channels carry water towards the fields.”

**Prajan:** “So the idea is not just to block the river. It is to guide the water!”

**Engineer:** “Exactly!”

### Scene 5 — A meeting with the king (30–35 sec)
**Visual:** A respectful, imagined Chola court. Karikala Chola listens to the engineer and Prajan.

**Prajan:** “Your Majesty, why is this work so important?”

**Karikala Chola (fictional dialogue):** “A kingdom grows strong when its people can live well. Water for farms means food for families.”

**Narrator:** “Kallanai is traditionally attributed to Karikala Chola. This conversation is an imaginative reconstruction.”

### Scene 6 — Back to the present (20–25 sec)
**Visual:** Golden light returns Prajan to present-day Kallanai. He looks at the river with new understanding.

**Prajan:** “Now I understand! Our ancestors studied the river and found a clever way to guide its water to the fields.”

**Narrator:** “Kallanai reminds us that thoughtful engineering can serve people for generations.”

**End card:** “Kallanai — an ancient water-diversion structure on the Kaveri. Learn, imagine, and explore!”

## Visual continuity notes

- Use the approved Prajan reference image/cutout as the source for Prajan. Do not regenerate his face when identity consistency matters.
- Keep his clothing and appearance consistent between scenes unless a parent/guardian approves a deliberate costume change.
- Generate backgrounds and historical characters separately, then composite Prajan into the scene.
- Ancient setting should be plausible and respectful: riverbank, stonework, simple tools, cotton garments, period-inspired architecture. Avoid fantasy armour or modern objects.
- Do not show a modern concrete barrage, vehicles, electric equipment, or modern clothing in ancient scenes.
