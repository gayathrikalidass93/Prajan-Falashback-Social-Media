# Production workflow

Use this workflow for every Prajan episode. Keep the parent/guardian as the final approver before anything is published.

## Episode stages

1. **Idea** — Record the historical topic, audience age range, and one learning goal.
2. **Research** — Collect trustworthy references. Separate confirmed facts, debated details, and creative reconstruction.
3. **Outline** — Plan the hook, time-travel moment, discovery, explanation, and takeaway.
4. **Script** — Write narration and dialogue in short, age-appropriate sentences.
5. **Scene planning** — Break the script into numbered scenes with duration, visual action, dialogue, and sound.
6. **Asset creation** — Create or source backgrounds, illustrations, voiceover, music, and effects. Keep approved character references consistent.
7. **Edit** — Assemble the video, captions, sound, and transitions.
8. **Review** — Check historical accuracy, child safety, accessibility, spelling, audio, and rights/permissions.
9. **Publish** — Parent/admin approves the final video, title, description, thumbnail, and destination.
10. **Learn** — Record performance and audience feedback without collecting unnecessary information about children.

## Suggested status labels

Backlog · Researching · Script draft · Script approved · Scene prompts ready · Assets in progress · Editing · Review · Approved · Published

## Per-episode checklist

- [ ] Learning goal is clear.
- [ ] Historical facts have sources.
- [ ] Creative reconstruction is clearly labelled in the internal notes and, where needed, in the video.
- [ ] Script is suitable for the intended age group.
- [ ] Character appearance and approved assets are consistent.
- [ ] Music, images, and footage have appropriate usage rights.
- [ ] Captions/subtitles are checked.
- [ ] No private or identifying child information appears.
- [ ] Parent/guardian has approved the final cut and publishing details.
